Why glueless wigs stand out

Ashimary's current homepage emphasizes simplifying wig wear with glueless and pre-styled options. For someone new to wigs, a ready-to-wear design can reduce installation work before styling.

Understanding the Invisi Strap 360 collection

The Invisi Strap 360 collection combines 360-degree lace construction with an adjustable strap system intended to provide a secure fit without traditional glue. Fit and appearance can vary, so check the exact listing details before ordering.

How to choose an Ashimary wig

1. Decide how much installation you want

Compare glueless, wear-and-go and pre-styled options with lace-front or full-lace constructions depending on how much styling control you want.

2. Choose the texture

Straight, body wave, loose wave, deep wave, curly and kinky textures create different looks and maintenance routines.

3. Check length and density

Length and density affect appearance, weight and styling. Read the specifications for the exact unit.

4. Consider lace and cap construction

Compare lace type, parting area, cap size, straps and pre-cut or pre-plucked details.

5. Review the exact product page

Prices, promotions, stock, shipping and specifications can change, so verify current details on the individual listing.
